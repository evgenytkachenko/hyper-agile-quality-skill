# Relationship to *Hyper-Agile Testing*

This skill applies the Hyper-Agile methodology as written in the methodology files of the
author's hyper-agile-engine, shipped here unchanged in `src/haq/_methodology/`:

- the ten activities of the Hyper-Agile Quality Loop (`references/quality-loop.md`)
- the six causal layers, the root-cause quality check and the "earliest reasonable missed
  opportunity" safeguard against hindsight bias (`references/causal-model.md`)
- the evidence confidence levels, the six test-coverage failure modes, action quality rules
  and "always identify what worked" (`references/evidence-and-actions.md`)
- the read-only, portable investigation phases (`SKILL.md`)

What this project does **not** do:

- It does not reproduce or quote text from the book.
- It does not refer to chapters, sections or page numbers of the book.
- It does not claim that a particular rule or term appears in the book in this form; the
  methodology files are an operational rendering written for tools, not an excerpt.

Readers who want the reasoning behind the methodology should read the book itself.
